## Transfer rules and evidence

Carry forward plot-to-chrome proportion, dense but readable text, aligned numeric evidence, distinct structural/grid/data layers, restrained effects and exact inspection. Keep authored curves, decorative map points, illustrative lengths and fixed tooltip positions out of general analytical algorithms.

Map each evidence role to a registered family or an explicitly scoped primitive before claiming coverage. A styled screenshot does not prove a data schema, a working editor field, keyboard access or a query/persistence path. The [capability notes](chart-capabilities.md) name the actual owners; the [iteration-06 report](iteration-06-interaction.md) retains the prior executed behavior, and the [iteration-07 report](iteration-07-complex-lenses.md) tracks the new concept implementations and pending validation.

Implementation screenshots belong in `docs/screenshots/` and must be identified by iteration and actual state. Retained source references belong in the reference directories above. Keep those two kinds of evidence distinct when comparing results or updating the skill.
